# Toast

## IPA Pronunciation
- /toʊst/

## Simple English Explanation
- Toast is bread that has been browned and made crispy by heating it. It is often eaten for breakfast or as a snack, and you can put things like butter, jam, or avocado on it.

## Example Sentences
- I like to have toast with butter and jam every morning.
- She made a delicious avocado toast for brunch.
- After burning the bread, he decided to make toast instead.
